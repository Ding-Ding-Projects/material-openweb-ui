Open WebUI, rebuilt in Material 3

Every model you run, wrapped in Material 3.

A frameless Windows desktop app for Open WebUI. Your chats, your models, your files and your one-time codes stay on the machine you are sitting at — no server to start, no account to make, and nothing in it is ever for sale.

Nine destinations, one tab strip

Every surface is a tab you open, pin, group and close. On a phone the strip becomes the scrolling row above rather than eating the width it exists to reveal.

Chat
Streamed replies from a model running on this machine, with a chat list that searches by text or by pattern.
Ollama manager
The daemon, installed models, the full catalog with every tag, and pulls with real progress. Fit verdicts read your actual RAM and VRAM.
File converter
Type detected from the actual bytes. Anything unavailable stays visible with the exact dependency it is missing.
Authenticator
Real one-time codes for whatever accounts you like, computed locally. No account, no sync, no network.
